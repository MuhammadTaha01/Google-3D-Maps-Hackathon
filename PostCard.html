Why 3D Map Postcards?

Google Maps and Google Earth aren't new but 3D Photorealistic Maps are. The goal is to make the experience easier for users to access maps. Like anything we have to walk before we can run and 3DMapsPostcard gives people a baby step to experiment and share 3DMaps. It is especially a great way to make maps accessible to all ages.
